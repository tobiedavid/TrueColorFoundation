// What we are building

A mirror that shows
what mirrors cannot.

Most self-knowledge tools ask you how you feel. You think about it, construct an answer, and report back. But what you report is already edited. Already filtered. Already the version of yourself you're comfortable presenting.

True Color works differently. It measures how fast you respond to words — before the analytical mind arrives. It tracks where you hesitate. It follows the thread of what you haven't finished processing. And it shows you the shape of what it finds.

The real data is not in your answers. It is in where you pause.

Not a diagnosis. Not a score. Just a shape — your shape — that you cannot argue with because it came from your own nervous system in real time.

// How it works
01

One word. Two choices. No time to think.

Words appear one at a time. You tap left for negative, right for positive. The speed of your response is the signal. Fast means resolved. Slow means the thread worth following.

02

The system follows the thread.

Where you hesitate, the instrument branches — sending related words that probe the territory more precisely. Not randomly. Semantically. Following the shape of what remains unresolved.

03

You see your own shape.

A three-dimensional form built from your responses. Not interpreted. Not labeled. Just shown. You bring the intelligence. The shape shows you where to look.

04

You come back.

The same session in 30 days. In six months. In a year. Did the hesitation before a word shorten? Did the shape change? The drift across time is the evidence of transformation — or the honest evidence that nothing has shifted yet.

// What we believe
Free. Always.

The people who need self-knowledge tools most are exactly the people who cannot access clinical support. True Color Foundation is free for everyone. Not free with conditions. Free.

Your data belongs to you.

We are a nonprofit. We cannot sell your data for profit — this is a legal constraint, not a promise. Your shape is yours. Nothing leaves your device without your explicit consent.

No interpretation. No verdict.

The instrument shows you what it finds. It does not tell you what it means. That work is yours. We believe the act of seeing clearly is itself transformative — and that you know better than any algorithm what your own shape means.

The mirror cannot be bought.

No acquirer can purchase this foundation and turn it into an ad platform. No investor can pressure it toward monetization. The nonprofit structure protects the integrity of the instrument permanently.
